Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This policy covers two apps built and operated by the same developer: Pairings (a TCG tournament tracker) and TurnZero (a round timer for TCG events). They're separate apps with separate data — this single page explains both, with each section marked so it's clear which app it applies to.

If you have questions about either app or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact:

Email: beeftcg@gmail.com

What information is collected

PairingsAccount & profile

  • Email address (used to sign in)
  • Username and display name, if you set them
  • Home city, and its approximate coordinates if you provide it or use the "use my location" option — used only to match you with friends attending nearby events
  • Preferred language and notification settings

PairingsContent you create

  • Tournament/event entries you add (name, date, store, notes, links)
  • Results you log (placement, record, round-by-round matches)
  • Decks you create or import, including data pulled from a Piltover Archive link you provide (deck name, legend, card count — fetched from the public page you link to)
  • Friend connections (who you've added, and who's added you)

PairingsIf you connect Google Calendar

  • An access token used only to create/update calendar events you explicitly ask to sync — we don't read your existing calendar

TurnZeroWhat's collected

  • TurnZero has no accounts and no sign-in. There is no email, name, or personal profile collected at all.
  • Timers you create are identified only by a randomly generated code — not tied to your identity.
  • If you turn on push notifications for a specific timer, your device's push subscription is stored, linked only to that timer's random ID, not to you personally.

Both appsAutomatically, for push notifications to work

  • A push subscription token provided by your browser, used solely to deliver the notifications you've asked for. This is relayed through your browser's push service (operated by Google, Apple, or Mozilla, depending on your device) — we never see the content of push messages beyond what we send.

How this information is used

  • To operate the core features of each app — showing your events, syncing a shared timer, computing your stats, matching you with friends
  • To send notifications you've opted into (and only the categories you've left enabled, for Pairings)
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. Neither app shows ads or shares data with advertisers.

Who your information is shared with

  • Other users, within the app's own features: in Pairings, an accepted friend can see your "going" list, and — unless you turn this off in Account → Privacy — your event history, results, and decks. Someone you invite to an event, or who scans a Pairings share link/code, sees the specific event details you chose to share.
  • Service providers who host and run the app: Supabase (database and backend), and Vercel or Netlify (web hosting). These providers process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • Google, only if you explicitly connect Google Calendar in Pairings.
  • Piltover Archive, only in the sense that importing a deck fetches publicly available data from a link you provide — no personal data is sent to them.
  • We do not otherwise sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

  • Export: in Pairings, you can download a full copy of your data anytime from Account → "Export my data."
  • Deletion: to delete your account and all associated data from either app, email the contact address above. We'll delete it within a reasonable time. (TurnZero timers age out naturally since they aren't tied to any account; you can also just stop using a given timer.)
  • We retain data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service.

Children's privacy

Neither app is directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is stored with Supabase, using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls that restrict each account to its own data (and, where explicitly enabled, to accepted friends). No system is perfectly secure, but we don't take shortcuts here.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of either app after a change means you accept the update.
