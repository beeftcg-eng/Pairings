Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy covers two apps built and operated by the same developer: Pairings (a TCG tournament tracker) and TurnZero (a round timer for TCG events). They're separate apps with separate data — this single page explains both, with each section marked so it's clear which app it applies to.

If you have questions about either app or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact:

Email: beeftcg@gmail.com

Using Pairings is also covered by the Terms of Use.

What information is collected

PairingsAccount & profile

  • Email address (used to sign in)
  • Username and display name, if you set them
  • A profile picture, if you upload one (it is publicly viewable by link, like most profile pictures)
  • Home city, and its approximate coordinates if you provide it or use the "use my location" option — used to match you with friends attending nearby events and to search for official events near you (see "Official event finders" below)
  • Player IDs you save on your Player Card (Bandai, Konami, Pokémon, Wizards and others) — visible only to you
  • Preferred language and notification settings

PairingsContent you create

  • Tournament/event entries you add (name, date, store, notes, links)
  • Results you log (placement, record, round-by-round matches)
  • Decks you create or import, including details copied from Beef's Brewhouse if you import a deck from it (deck name, game, format, legend or leader, colors, card count)
  • Friend connections (who you've added, and who's added you), and playgroups you create or join
  • Your RSVPs ("going", "maybe") and, if you scan a store's check-in QR code, the time you checked in
  • Stores you follow, and, if you run a store in Pairings, its details, events and announcements

PairingsStored on your own device

  • A copy of your events and decks, so the app works without a connection and nothing is lost if the connection drops. It is removed when you sign out.
  • Your colour theme and a few display preferences. These never leave your device.
  • If you open a store's "Follow in Pairings" link before signing in, the store's follow code is kept on your device for up to a day, so the store can be followed once you've signed in.
  • Result cards (the shareable images of a result) are drawn on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share or save the image yourself.

PairingsIf you connect Beef's Brewhouse

  • Beef's Brewhouse (a deckbuilder by the same developer) runs on its own, separate Supabase project with its own accounts. If you log in to it from Pairings' Decks screen, that login session is stored in your browser and used only to read your Brewhouse deck list; it is removed when you disconnect or sign out of Pairings.
  • If you log in to Pairings from Brewhouse, Brewhouse reads the results you logged with decks you imported from it (event name, date, game, format, store, placement, record and matches), to show each deck's tournament record there. It can't change anything in Pairings.

PairingsIf you connect Google Calendar

  • An access token used only to create/update calendar events you explicitly ask to sync — we don't read your existing calendar

PairingsOfficial event finders and event links

"Riftbound near you", "One Piece near you" and "Magic near you" look up official events near your home city on the game publishers' own event sites, and pasting an event link reads that one event from the same sites. Only what's needed for the search is sent: your home city's approximate coordinates and the search radius (or the event number from the link). Your name, email and account are never sent.

  • Riftbound (Riot Games, playriftbound.com): the search goes through our own server, so Riot sees our server, not your device.
  • One Piece (Bandai, Bandai TCG+) and Magic: The Gathering (Wizards of the Coast, Wizards Store & Event Locator): your device asks their sites directly, so they also see your device's IP address, as with visiting their sites yourself.
  • Pokémon: events come from a daily copy we keep of pokedata.ovh's public list. Your search stays within Pairings.
  • An event you add from a finder is saved like any event you add yourself. Those sites' own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

PairingsOther services the app contacts

  • Have I Been Pwned (Pwned Passwords): when you choose a password, the app checks that it hasn't appeared in a known data breach. Only the first 5 characters of a one-way fingerprint (SHA-1 hash) of the password are sent; the password itself, and even its full fingerprint, never leave your device.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when you type a home city or an event address, that text is sent to OpenStreetMap to find its coordinates.
  • Card lists (riftcodex.com for Riftbound, optcgapi.com for One Piece): the app downloads the public list of leaders and legends to suggest names on the result form. Nothing about you is sent.
  • TurnZero: "Start a round timer" opens TurnZero with the event's name and game filled in.

PairingsUsage statistics

  • Page visits (Vercel Web Analytics): counts visits to the site without cookies and without identifying you: the page, the referring site, and your country, device type and browser. Addresses are sent without their query part, so share codes, follow codes and check-in links are never included.
  • Feature counts: the app adds 1 to a daily total when a feature is used (for example, opening an event finder or sharing a result card). Only the feature's name and the date are stored: not who used it.
  • These help decide what to improve. They are never used for advertising.

TurnZeroWhat's collected

  • TurnZero has no accounts and no sign-in. There is no email, name, or personal profile collected at all.
  • Timers you create are identified only by a randomly generated code — not tied to your identity.
  • If you turn on push notifications for a specific timer, your device's push subscription is stored, linked only to that timer's random ID, not to you personally.

Both appsAutomatically, for push notifications to work

  • A push subscription token provided by your browser, used solely to deliver the notifications you've asked for. This is relayed through your browser's push service (operated by Google, Apple, or Mozilla, depending on your device) — we never see the content of push messages beyond what we send.

How this information is used

  • To operate the core features of each app — showing your events, syncing a shared timer, computing your stats, matching you with friends
  • To send notifications you've opted into (and only the categories you've left enabled, for Pairings)
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. Neither app shows ads or shares data with advertisers.

Who your information is shared with

  • Other users, within the app's own features: in Pairings, an accepted friend can see your "going" list, and — unless you turn this off in Account → Privacy — your event history, results, and decks. Members of a playgroup you join see your username and are told when you're going to an event. Someone you invite to an event, or who opens a Pairings share link/code, sees the specific event details you chose to share (share codes expire after 30 days). Other users can see your username, display name and profile picture.
  • Store owners: if you RSVP to or check in at an event run by a store on Pairings, that store's owner can see your username and your RSVP or check-in for their own events, and can download that list.
  • Official event sites: your home city's approximate coordinates, as described under "Official event finders" above.
  • Service providers who host and run the app: Supabase (database and backend) and Vercel (web hosting and cookieless visit statistics). These providers process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • Google, only if you explicitly connect Google Calendar in Pairings.
  • Beef's Brewhouse, only if you connect it as described above; the data stays within your own accounts.
  • We do not otherwise sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

  • Export: in Pairings, you can download a full copy of your data anytime from Account → "Export my data."
  • Deletion: see "Delete your Pairings account" below. (TurnZero timers age out naturally since they aren't tied to any account; you can also just stop using a given timer.)
  • We retain data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. Share codes are removed after 30 days, and records of events or decks you deleted are kept for 60 days so the deletion reaches all your devices.

Delete your Pairings account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In the app: open ☰ → Account and tap "Delete my account" at the bottom. For your protection this only works if you signed in within the last 30 minutes; if it's been longer, the app asks you to sign out and back in first.
  • By email: write to beeftcg@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, profile picture, events, results, decks, player IDs, friends, playgroup memberships, RSVPs and check-ins, notifications and push subscriptions, and any Google Calendar connection. Stores you own (with their events) and playgroups you created are removed too. This can't be undone, so export your data first (Account → "Export my data") if you want a copy. Events you already added to your own Google Calendar stay there, and results you shared as images stay wherever you posted them.

Children's privacy

Neither app is directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is stored with Supabase, using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls that restrict each account to its own data (and, where explicitly enabled, to accepted friends). No system is perfectly secure, but we don't take shortcuts here.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of either app after a change means you accept the update.
